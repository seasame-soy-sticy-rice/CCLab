These changes suggest that the organism does not simply react to the present moment. Its current behavior also depends on its recent history. The body becomes a living display of an internal state that cannot be observed directly.

Stop interacting and observe how the organism gradually recovers. The creature's movement continues even in the absence of stimulation, but its response to you may change.
